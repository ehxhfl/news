[ZD Net Korea] One of the headaches of astronauts staying in space was laundry. You can't do laundry in space, so you have to wear the same clothes for weeks. It was common for clothes worn in space to be brought to Earth in sealed bags and either washed or burned upon entering the atmosphere. There was no problem using this method when performing short-term space missions. However, this was one of the biggest concerns during a Mars exploration mission that took several months or more. Science media outlet Space.com recently reported that a new washing method has been developed to relieve astronauts' long-standing concerns. According to the report, researchers led by Professor Gabe Xu of the University of Alabama Hunsville have developed a prototype 'plasma washing gun' that can wash clothes in outer space.
