[ZD Net Korea] On July 30th, local time, six bipartisan senators, including U.S. Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer, sent a letter to Apple CEO Tim Cook, demanding that Apple not use Chinese memory semiconductors. The targets are Changshin Memory (CXMT) and Yangtze Memory (YMTC). Lawmakers demanded assurances that products from both companies would not be included in any Apple products around the world, including those for the Chinese market. The letter was led by Schumer and Representatives Jim Banks and signed by Representatives Andy Kim, Jan Shaheen, Mike Crapo, and Pete Ricketts. There are three Democratic and Republican lawmakers each. Three reasons were presented. The Department of Defense has designated the two companies as Chinese military enterprises, Yangtze Memory has been placed on the Commerce Department's export control list in 2022, and procurement by federal agencies is prohibited starting in December 2027 under the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023. The letter also includes a high-tech packaging plant being built by SK Hynix in West Lafayette, Indiana.
